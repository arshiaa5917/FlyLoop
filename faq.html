Travel made simpler

Frequently Asked Questions

FlyLoop is designed around one simple idea: make travel feel as easy as a monthly membership. Flights, hotels, eligible stays and support are planned together so members can spend less time organizing and more time enjoying the trip.

Flights + hotels included Every membership is planned around bundled travel essentials.
Stays up to 14 days Members can use included stays for short breaks or longer trips.
24/7 trip + visa advisors Support is part of the design, from planning questions to travel documents.
Is FlyLoop available for real bookings right now?
FlyLoop is currently being presented as a travel subscription concept. The website shows the planned membership experience, destination structure and booking flow before full live booking operations are launched.
What is included with a membership?
All memberships are designed to include flights, hotels, access to every FlyLoop destination, stays up to 14 days, and 24/7 trip and visa advisor support. The goal is to make travel feel organized, predictable and hassle-free.
How do the three plans work?
  • Basic Loop - 2 trips per year.
  • Standard Loop - 3 trips per year.
  • Premium Loop - 5 trips per year.
Each plan includes flights, hotels, stays up to 14 days and access to all FlyLoop destinations. Higher tiers add more trips, more date flexibility and stronger booking priority.
Which destinations are included?
FlyLoop currently focuses on Toronto, Montreal, Vancouver, Los Angeles, New York, Miami, Cancun and Las Vegas. Every membership tier is designed to have access to the same destination list.
What do trip and visa advisors help with?
Advisors are planned to be available 24/7 to help with trip questions, destination guidance, travel document reminders, visa preparation basics, booking updates and unexpected travel issues. FlyLoop is designed to reduce the stress that usually comes with planning and managing a trip.
Can members choose their travel dates?
Members would request preferred dates inside their plan. FlyLoop would then match those requests with available flights, hotel partners and destination inventory. Premium members would receive stronger priority when demand is high.
Can a membership be paused or cancelled?
The planned membership model would include clear cancellation rules, trip credit timing and pause options. The goal is to keep the subscription flexible while still allowing FlyLoop to reserve flights, hotels and advisor support reliably.
Why use FlyLoop instead of booking everything separately?
FlyLoop is built for people who want travel to feel predictable. Instead of comparing flights, hotels, dates and travel requirements every time, members get one membership, curated destinations and support before, during and after the trip.
