How FlyLoop Works

FlyLoop turns travel planning into a simple membership. Choose your plan, request your trip, and let FlyLoop organize the key pieces: flights, hotels, eligible stays, and support when you need it.

Your member journey

1

Choose a membership

Pick Basic, Standard or Premium based on how many trips you want each year.

2

Select your destination

Browse the FlyLoop city list and choose where you want your next trip to go.

3

Request your travel window

Share your preferred dates so FlyLoop can match flights, hotels and availability.

4

Get your trip organized

Flights, hotels and stays up to 14 days are planned together under your membership.

5

Travel with support

24/7 trip and visa advisors help with questions, travel document reminders and updates.

6

Keep the loop going

After your trip, your membership continues toward your next eligible escape.
